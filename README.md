# Ferns and Petals Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project analyzes sales data from **Ferns and Petals (FNP)**, a gifting company that serves customers across various occasions such as Diwali, Raksha Bandhan, Holi, Valentine’s Day, Birthdays, and Anniversaries.

The goal of this analysis is to understand revenue trends, customer behavior, product performance, and delivery patterns so the company can improve sales strategy and customer satisfaction.

## Problem Statement
Ferns and Petals wanted to analyze its sales dataset to uncover meaningful insights related to:
- Total revenue
- Average order and delivery time
- Monthly sales performance
- Top products by revenue
- Customer spending analysis
- Sales performance of top products
- Top cities by number of orders
- Relationship between order quantity and delivery time
- Revenue comparison across occasions
- Product popularity by occasion

## Objectives
- Identify revenue trends across months and occasions
- Find top-performing products and cities
- Analyze customer spending and delivery behavior
- Build an interactive Excel dashboard for business decision-making

## Dataset Description
The dataset contains information related to:
- Orders
- Customers
- Products
- Occasions
- Order dates
- Delivery dates
- Revenue and order details

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers
- Data Cleaning
- Dashboard Design

## Key Business Questions Answered
1. What is the total revenue?
2. What is the average order and delivery time?
3. How do sales vary month by month in 2023?
4. Which products generate the highest revenue?
5. How much do customers spend on average?
6. Which products perform best in sales?
7. Which cities place the most orders?
8. Does higher order quantity affect delivery time?
9. Which occasions generate the most revenue?
10. Which products are most popular for each occasion?

## Project Workflow
1. Cleaned and prepared the dataset in Excel
2. Created calculated fields for analysis
3. Built pivot tables for summary insights
4. Designed charts for revenue, products, cities, and occasions
5. Added slicers for interactive filtering
6. Created a dashboard to present findings visually

## Key Insights
- The total revenue generated was **₹35,20,984**.
- The dashboard shows clear variation in sales across different months and occasions.
- A few products contribute a major share of revenue.
- Certain cities place significantly more orders than others.
- Customer spending and delivery time can be used to improve business operations.
- Occasion-based analysis helps identify seasonal demand patterns.

## Dashboard Features
- Total Orders KPI
- Total Revenue KPI
- Average Order Delivery Time KPI
- Average Customer Spend KPI
- Revenue by Occasion
- Revenue by Category
- Revenue by Month
- Revenue by Hour
- Top 5 Products by Revenue
- Top 10 Cities by Orders
- Interactive slicers for date, order date, and occasion

## Business Recommendations
- Focus more promotions on high-revenue occasions.
- Stock and advertise top-selling products before peak seasons.
- Improve delivery planning in high-order cities.
- Use customer spending patterns to create better offers and bundles.
- Monitor delivery time to improve customer satisfaction.
